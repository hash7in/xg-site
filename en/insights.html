Equipment & lines06

The five lines we build, plus a separate page for plastics as a feedstock.

BATCH PYROLYSIS
01

Waste Tyre Batch Pyrolysis Plant

Batch pyrolysis in a horizontal rotary reactor: whole tyres with a bead cut, 1–45 TPD, 45–50% oil yield, saleable wire and carbon black.

Read the note →
WASTE PLASTICS
02

Waste Plastic Pyrolysis Plant

Waste plastics run on the batch pyrolysis line. Household plastic, film, bales and alu-plastic can be assessed. Sri Lanka, Peru and China projects are on record.

Read the note →
SKID-MOUNTED
03

Skid-Mounted Pyrolysis Plant

Reactor, condenser, gas train and control panel on one base: 1–4 TPD, running on arrival. Built for pilot runs and distributed collection points.

Read the note →
CONTINUOUS PYROLYSIS
04

Continuous Pyrolysis Plant

Nitrogen purged, continuous feed and discharge, sealed slag-out at temperature. Standard 30 / 40 / 50 t/d. YD-C30 and YD-C45 figures are on this page.

Read the note →
DISTILLATION
05

Waste Oil Distillation Plant

Atmospheric or vacuum distillation for waste lube, tyre oil and plastic oil. 75–90% diesel-range yield, 8–30 TPD, no process wastewater.

Read the note →
CARBONIZATION
06

Continuous Biomass Carbonization Plant

Continuous carbonization for rice husk, straw, sawdust, coconut shell, palm kernel shell and wood chips at 1–3 t/h. Gas-and-char co-generation: self-fuelled, one third of the heat runs the line, two thirds dry the feed.

Read the note →

How these pages are written

Capacities, yields, lead times and the project list come from the equipment schedule and the brochure. Where we have no measured data the page says "calculated per project" instead of filling in a plausible number. Client names are partly anonymised in the brochure; site photos and proposal originals can be reviewed at the factory.
