02PROCESS & SAFETY

PROCESS & SAFETY

Sealed, oxygen-free, slightly negative — safety designed in, not watched in.

  1. 01Nitrogen purgeNitrogen generator purges oxygen out of the reactor
  2. 02Heat-upOil or off-gas firing, 2–5 h ramp to about 400 °C
  3. 03Sealed feedingAutomatic feeder, sealed feed section
  4. 04SeparationSpray header → settling tank drops heavy oil → condensers make fuel oil
  5. 05Off-gas reuseTwo water seals plus flame arrestor into the hot-air furnace
  6. 06Flue-gas cleaningTwo-stage spray scrubbing → induced-draft fan → stack
  7. 07Sealed dischargeFully automatic sealed discharge, no leakage

Control points

Air ingressSeals + slight negative pressure + leak test + nitrogen connection
Off-gas flashbackWater seal + flame arrestor + check valve + interlock
OverpressurePressure monitoring + alarm + relief + emergency shut-off
Cooling water lossFlow and pump-status alarm + safe shutdown logic
Residue self-ignitionFull cooling + door temperature limit + sealed conveying
Power / fan failureFuel cut-off + safe valve position + emergency power

Emission guarantees follow local standards — we commit to meeting limits with desulphurization and dedusting (DeNOx optional), never to "inherently compliant".

03OUTPUTS & APPLICATIONS

One tonne of waste tyres, four sellable outputs.

≈45% Pyrolysis oil Boiler / kiln fuel oil; distilled into diesel-range industrial fuel
≈38% Carbon residue / rCB Co-firing; steelmaking recarburiser; upgraded to recovered carbon black for rubber
Tyre dependent Recovered steel wire Lifted out by grab, sold to steel mills or metal recyclers; baled after de-rubbering
≈9% Non-condensable gas Recovered and fired under the drum to heat later batches, cutting external fuel demand sharply
