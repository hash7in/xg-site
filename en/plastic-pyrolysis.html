WASTE PLASTICS

Waste Plastic Pyrolysis Plant

Same batch platform as tyres. Plastic is sized on its own — tyre oil-yield figures are not reused.

Plastics we assess

Plastics listed in the brochure: mixed household, film and packaging, baled feedstock, pharmaceutical or PV-backsheet alu-plastic, and paper-mill rejects. The reactor and pre-treatment change with the feed. Send a sample or a lab sheet; selection and throughput estimates are not charged extra.

The plant

Batch pyrolysis covers 1–45 TPD, one charge per batch, about 20 hours. The 45–50% oil yield on the tyre page is for tyres only. Plastic yield depends on the resin and is not given as one number here.

On record: Sri Lanka, Ecolux Bio Harvest, batch 15 / 50 TPD, waste plastics; Peru, Peru Bioquimicos, batch 2 / 10 TPD, waste plastics; China, Fujian / Henan, batch 15 TPD ×4 sets, plastics / tyres.
